# Marketing copy

Read the relevant rendering source, [`docs/agent-decision-councils.md`](../../../docs/agent-decision-councils.md), and
[`docs/public-contributor-safety.md`](../../../docs/public-contributor-safety.md).

- Use plain, specific claims about the firm and its work; avoid comparisons, superlatives, guarantees, and unsupported
  outcome or certification claims.
- Verify every concrete fact with its canonical repository source or the user; do not infer it from nearby copy.
- Convene the Legal Council for legal-copy decisions and the Client Council for visitor-facing decisions.
- Do not expose client facts, legal files, real personal contact details, or production identifiers in public copy.
