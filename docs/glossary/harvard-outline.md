---
title: "Harvard Outline"
---

The numbering used on motions and contracts so a provision is addressable by path (`I`, `I.A`, `1.B`). Depth-1 headings
are Roman numerals (`I.`, `II.`) on contracts and closing letters, or Arabic numerals (`1.`, `2.`) in motion practice.
Lettered subsections (`A.`, `B.`) are Markdown block quotes. `views::harvard_outline` parses that shape into narration
units. A notation the firm has given a client narrates on that stage at `/app/projects/{code}/{notation_id}/outline`.
`N123` holds every kind that must carry an outline to its depth-1 scheme.
