---
title: "Counsel"
description: "Counsel means an attorney."
---

An **attorney** — spelled c-o-u-n-s-e-l. The members of the [Legal Council](council.md) are counsels; "ethics counsel,"
"Senior Counsel," and "trial counsel" all use this spelling. Outside counsel working one of the firm's matters is a
`lawyer` [Person](person.md), not a separate [Participation](participation.md) word. Distinct from [Council](council.md)
(c-o-u-n-c-i-l), which is the *group*: the Legal Council is a council of counsels. [Navigator MCP](navigator-mcp.md) is
the agent that carries the Legal Council tool — it is neither a counsel nor the name of the council.
