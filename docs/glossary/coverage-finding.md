---
title: "Coverage Finding"
---

One assessment of whether an [Inquiry](inquiry.md) has been answered during a Live Inquiry Session. A finding may be
model-authored or lawyer-authored, and it cites transcript evidence; it is not a confirmed
[Answer](../notation.md#answer) until a lawyer turns it into one.

- Design: [`live-inquiry-coverage`](../live-inquiry-coverage.md)
