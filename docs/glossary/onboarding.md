title: "Onboarding"
---

The codebase term for the notation that **opens a matter** — `rules::kind::Kind::Onboarding`, classified by
[`Kind::opens_a_matter`](../../rules/src/kind.rs). The shipped sample is the onboarding letter (`onboarding__letter`).
One kind covers that act of opening the matter.

In conversation and with clients this is the **onboarding letter** — see [Engagement /
Retainer](engagement--retainer.md) for that client-facing shape. The codebase and the workflow runtime speak Onboarding.
